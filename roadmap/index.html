Roadmap

What we are building

The roadmap lives in GitHub issues: vote for an item with a 👍, tell us your use case in a comment, or suggest something new. This page follows the issues every day (last change 2026-10-09). Shipped work goes to the changelog.

In progress1

being built now

  • Source citations in every episode

    Every line of a script points to the source it came from, and the transcript shows it, so a listener can check any claim. Scripts already carry the citations; next is showing them in the transcript and on the episode page.

Next3

up after that

  • Saved source trays

    Save a mix of feeds and notes as a template that a watchlist refills on a schedule, so a recurring show needs no clicks.

    #30👍 0vote or comment

  • Longer, smoother episodes

    Get closer to NotebookLM in length and flow, measured in the public blind test against NotebookLM and Open Notebook rather than guessed.

    #31👍 0vote or comment

  • More voice engines, including commercial ones (ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Google, Azure)

    The commercial top voices as engines you can switch on per show, with your own API key, so a show can sound as good as the best voices available today. Local engines stay the default.

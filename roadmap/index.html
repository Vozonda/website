Roadmap

What we are building

The roadmap lives in GitHub issues: vote for an item with a 👍, tell us your use case in a comment, or suggest something new. This page follows the issues every day (last change 2026-10-08). Shipped work goes to the changelog.

In progress

  • Source citations in every episodeEach line of a script points to the source it came from, and the transcript shows it, so a listener can check any claim. Multi-source episodes already cite every content turn (see CHANGELOG, Unreleased); what remains is showing the citations in the transcript and the episode page.👍 0

Next

  • Saved source traysSave a mix of feeds and notes as a template that a watchlist refills on a schedule, so a recurring show needs no clicks.👍 0
  • Longer, smoother episodesGet closer to NotebookLM in length and flow. Measured, not guessed: the public blind test at https://vozonda.com/test/ compares Vozonda, NotebookLM and Open Notebook on the same sources.👍 0
  • More voice engines, including commercial ones (ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Google, Azure)The first wish from a potential user after the launch: more voice engines, including the commercial top models, so a show can sound as good as the best voices available today.👍 0

Later

  • Vozonda CloudA hosted version, paid per episode with Lightning and without accounts, if enough people ask for it. The waitlist is one anonymous click on https://vozonda.com/#cloud.👍 0
  • Hosts with a stanceGive each host a position on a topic, so a debate is a real debate instead of two people agreeing politely.👍 0

Ideas

  • Nostr bookmarks as a sourceBookmark an article in your Nostr app and it becomes an episode, read from your public bookmark list (NIP-51). Not built yet; the About and FAQ pages described it by mistake until #24.👍 0
